Local Mushaf pages are downloaded during the Android build from quran-ws/quran-svg v1.1.1 (Hafs/KFGQPC). The pages include the ayah polygon layer used for tap selection and audio highlighting. Source: https://github.com/quran-ws/quran-svg. The underlying KFGQPC Madani Mushaf artwork remains subject to the King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex terms. The quran-ws polygon/metadata contribution is documented in that repository.
